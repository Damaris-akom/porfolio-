My Customer Success story

I started in client service, handling complaints with clear case logs and writing message templates that raised client confidence in issue resolution by 20%. That taught me that clients stay when they feel heard and know what happens next.

From there I moved into onboarding, running 20+ client accounts at once and fixing blockers directly with technical and network teams. As a Customer Success Specialist and later Account Manager, I took on renewals, upsell conversations and business reviews with senior decision-makers.

Today I combine relationship management with data. I use CRM records, usage data and dashboards to decide where to act, and I keep compliance and technical teams aligned so clients are never left guessing.

What clients can expect from me

  • Clear communication, written and spoken, at every stage
  • Early action on inactive and at-risk accounts
  • Expectations set honestly, escalations handled calmly
  • Feedback passed to the right team and followed through
  • Organised records, follow-ups and account reporting
